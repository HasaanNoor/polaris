# Income and Health Illustrative Context

This repository-authored illustrative document is not a published academic paper.

Economic development research often discusses associations between national income,
health-system capacity, nutrition, sanitation, and life expectancy. In cross-country
settings, higher GDP per capita is frequently treated as a socioeconomic context
variable when examining population health outcomes.

## Interpretation Limits

Income and life expectancy comparisons should not be treated as causal proof without
research designs that address confounding, measurement differences, and temporal order.
